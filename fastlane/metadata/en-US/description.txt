Color 100 animal stencils, including 60 with “tiger” in the title. Pick from 14 ink colors, adjust the brush size, and tap or draw on the canvas. Turn on Stay inside the lines to keep each stroke within the area where it starts. Erase, undo or redo edits, or clear the page after confirming.

Open the Sketchbook to search stencil titles and subtitles, filter for tigers, or find pages you’ve worked on. The grid shows previews of your saved artwork. Pick an image from your photo library or take a picture with the camera to turn it into an outlined stencil you can color.

Save your framed artwork and its title as a PNG to Files or Photos. Tiger Press runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. The app interface is in English.
