Colour 100 animal stencils, including 60 with ‘tiger’ in the title. Choose from 14 ink colours, adjust the brush size and tap or draw on the page. Turn on Stay inside the lines to keep each stroke within the area where it starts. Use the eraser, undo or redo edits, or clear the page after confirming.

Open the Sketchbook to search stencil titles and subtitles, browse tiger designs or see pages you’ve worked on. The grid shows previews of your saved artwork. Choose a photo from your library or take one with the camera to turn it into an outlined stencil you can colour.

Export your framed artwork and its title as a PNG to Files or Photos. Tiger Press runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. The app interface is in English.
